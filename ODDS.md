# Falling Animation — Spawn Odds

Every falling item (petals, leaves, hearts) rolls for a kind, then hearts roll for a colour or a rare special.

## Per Drop

| Item | Chance |
|---|---|
| White petal | 45% |
| Green leaf | 15% |
| 💙 deep blue heart | ~12.6% |
| 🩵 light blue heart | ~12.6% |
| 🤍 white heart | ~12.6% |
| 💖 or 💝 | ~0.8% |
| 💎 | 0.04% |

## Heart Breakdown (within the 40% that is a heart)

| Type | Chance |
|---|---|
| 💙 / 🩵 / 🤍 (normal) | ~98.9% |
| 💖 / 💝 | ~1% |
| 💎 | 0.1% |

## Fun facts

- 💎 appears about **1 in 1,150** drops.
- 💖 / 💝 appear about **1 in 125** drops.
- The other ~9 in 10 drops are the soft white petals and green leaves that set the mood.
